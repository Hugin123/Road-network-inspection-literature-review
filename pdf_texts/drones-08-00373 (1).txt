This study focused on the routing of surveillance drones considering their energy
capacities,whichareconsumedduringbothservicinganddeadheading. TheDARP-DD
anditsspecialcase,theMS-DARP-DD,werediscussed. Thesimulatedannealingalgorithm
wasusedtosolvetheseproblems. Thecomponentsofthesimulatedannealingalgorithm,
includingsolutionrepresentation,neighborsearchoperators,constrainthandlingstrategy,
andparametervalues,weredesignedinaccordancewiththeproblems.

required edges. The k-route shorten, smart edge direction change, and smart reverse

thealgorithmasaconstraint-handlingstrategy.
