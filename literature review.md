# Literature Review: Arc Routing Problems (ARP) with UAV Applications (2020-2026)

## Overview
This document summarizes recent academic literature (2020–2026) on Arc Routing Problems (ARP) and their variants (e.g., CARP, MCARP) with a focus on Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV/Drone) applications. The review is based on searches from arXiv, covering high‑impact papers relevant to the field. Each entry includes the authors, year, specific problem variant addressed, core algorithmic approach, main contribution, and APA‑style reference.

## Summary of Trends

1. **Integration of Drones in Arc Routing**: Recent works extend classic arc‑routing problems (RPP, CARP) to incorporate drones as auxiliary or primary vehicles, often in tandem with ground trucks.
2. **Algorithmic Diversity**: Solutions range from exact MILP formulations to metaheuristics (genetic algorithms, GRASP) and learning‑based methods (vision‑language models for UAV path planning).
3. **Real‑World Constraints**: Models increasingly consider battery‑life, speed‑dependent energy consumption, temporal unavailabilities, and stochastic demands.
4. **Multi‑Agent Coordination**: Several papers study fleets of trucks and drones, focusing on coordinated launch‑and‑recover operations to minimize makespan or total cost.
5. **Beyond Traditional ARP**: Some works address related routing challenges in UAV networks (ad‑hoc routing, swarm security) that can inform arc‑routing models for inspection and delivery tasks.

## Limitations

This review is based primarily on arXiv preprints and a selection of conference/journal papers available through open archives. It does not cover the full breadth of literature behind paywalls (e.g., IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect). Future updates should include searches of those databases for a more comprehensive survey.
